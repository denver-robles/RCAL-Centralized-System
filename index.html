About Us

ARCHDIOCESE OF LIPA

The Archdiocese of Lipa is a local Church of the Catholic faith. It serves the faithful in the province of Batangas. The Metropolitan Cathedral of St. Sebastian in Lipa City serves as our main seat and cathedral.
