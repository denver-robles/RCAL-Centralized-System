Canonical Guidelines

Document Requirements by Sacrament

Review canonical prerequisites and required documents before filing your certificate claim or scheduling a rite.

📜 For Certificate Requests
  • Exact full name of candidate and date of birth
  • Parents' full names (including mother's maiden surname)
  • Parish where the baptism took place
  • Approximate year or date of ceremony
📅 For Scheduling a Baptism
  • PSA Birth Certificate of child
  • Parents' Catholic Marriage Contract (or civil certificate)
  • Confirmation certificate of principal sponsors/godparents
  • Attendance at parish Pre-Jordan Catechetical Seminar
📜 For Certificate Requests
  • Candidate's full name as recorded in the baptismal registry
  • Name of officiating bishop or parish priest
  • Year of confirmation and parish church location
  • Valid government-issued ID of requester
📅 For Scheduling a Confirmation
  • Recent Baptismal Certificate marked "For Confirmation"
  • Sponsor's Catholic confirmation record
  • Catechetical seminar completion slip
  • Parish vicar or parish priest endorsement
📜 For Certificate Requests
  • Full names of groom and bride (including bride's maiden name)
  • Date and church where the marriage was solemnized
  • Canonical purpose (e.g. legal, passport, child baptism)
  • Proof of kinship if claimed on behalf of spouse
📅 For Scheduling a Wedding
  • PSA Birth Certificates & CENOMAR of both parties
  • Baptismal & Confirmation certificates marked "For Marriage"
  • Canonical interview with Parish Priest & Pre-Cana seminar
  • Publication of Church Banns for three consecutive Sundays
📜 For Certificate Requests
  • Full name of the deceased
  • Date of death and date of church funeral blessing
  • Parish where the funeral Mass or burial was recorded
  • Immediate family authorization
📅 For Scheduling a Funeral
  • Official Death Certificate (duly registered)
  • Burial permit from municipal health office
  • Coordination with cemetery or columbarium
  • Parish priest blessing schedule coordination

Frequently Asked Questions & Chancery Helpdesk

Standard document requests (Baptism, Confirmation, and Marriage certificates) are typically processed and released within 3 to 5 business days following canonical book search and seal inscription. Urgent requests may be accommodated depending on parish volume.

To protect personal and sacramental data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, requests can only be filed by the document owner or direct family members (parents, legal guardians). Please present one government-issued photo ID (e.g. Passport, UMID, Driver's License, National ID).

Matrimony schedules require at least 2 to 3 months lead time for canonical interviews, Pre-Cana formation, and marriage banns. Infant baptisms require submission of the child's PSA birth certificate and completion of the pre-baptismal catechesis.
