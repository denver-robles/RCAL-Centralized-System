The Archdiocese of Lipa serves the faithful in the province of Batangas. Modernized PIMS consolidates sacramental registers, certificate generation, and document requests into one trusted platform, keeping sensitive canonical records protected under RA 10173 while making everyday parish service faster, verified, and transparent.
