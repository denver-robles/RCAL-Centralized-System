Password Recovery Assistance

PARISHIONER ACCOUNT RECOVERY

How to Reset Your Parishioner Password

Under archdiocesan data privacy policies (RA 10173), password resets require verified account ownership:

Step 1: Email or visit your home parish office or the Archdiocesan Chancery.
Step 2: Present or attach a scanned copy of a valid government-issued ID matching your registered name.
Step 3: Parish staff will verify your sacramental records and issue a secure temporary access credential.
